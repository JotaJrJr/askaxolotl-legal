Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Ask Axolotl (“the app”) is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It has no hosted backend and no database of users. This policy explains what happens to data when the owner connects a TikTok account.

1. What the app accesses

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the app requests only the permissions it needs:

  • user.info.basic – to show which account is connected (display name and avatar).
  • video.upload and video.publish – to send a video the owner has created and confirmed, as a draft or as a post.

2. What is stored, and where

The access and refresh tokens that TikTok issues are saved in a file on the owner's own computer, so the owner does not have to sign in each time. They are not sent to anyone else. The owner can delete that file, or revoke the app's access in TikTok under Settings → Security & permissions, at any time.

Videos, cover images and their text are created and kept on the owner's computer.

3. What is shared

The app does not sell, rent or share personal data. Data leaves the owner's computer only for these purposes: sending a video the owner has confirmed to TikTok, and (optionally) requesting quiz text from an AI text service, which receives the quiz topic and not any TikTok account data.

4. Other people's data

The app does not collect, store or process personal data of other TikTok users.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.
